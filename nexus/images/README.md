# Images

Nexus page images, tracked in the repo. The background is 1300x372, the gallery images are 1920x1080. The mods-grid thumbnail is not a separate file: Nexus picks it from the uploaded gallery images. The images were prepared by hand from in-game screenshots of the HUD trap list, the same save without and with the mod. The 1.2.0 images came at 1922x1083 and were cropped to 1920x1080 by their black edges. The trap icon on the title card is the game's own HUD trap icon (an inline SVG in `CoreUI1.html`).

- `banner.jpg` - the mod page background image (1300x372): the game's trap list without the mod (one long row for each trap, faded at the bottom) and an arrow to the Trapline grid with the "Take All" button.
- `sl-trapline-thumbnail.jpg` - title card (1920x1080): the "Trapline" wordmark and the trap icon beside the 1.2.0 grid: the floor lines, the Outdoors zone line, a free slot, and the last line with the Auto-replace checkbox and the pin. Pick this as the mods-grid thumbnail.
- `sl-trapline-auto-replace.jpg` - "Auto-replace Broken Traps" (1920x1080): the grid with two red cells ("Red cell: breaks at the next pickup") and the Auto-replace checkbox ("Sets a new trap of the same kind, from your backpack or home storage"). The pin in this shot is the lock of the in-game review, not the released push pin.
- `sl-trapline-set-trap.jpg` - "Quick Trap Setup" (1920x1080): the grid with the Take All button, a free slot with the plus, and the open Set Trap menu with three trap kinds and their counts ("Click on a free Trap Slot, then pick a trap").
- `sl-trapline-comparison.jpg` - before and after (1920x1080): the full HUD with the game's trap list ("Original"), which fills the screen, beside the HUD with the Trapline grid ("Mod").
